# Creation from a primordial state

What existed first, and what does each text say changes?

Status: INITIAL COMPARISON.

## Compare passages of comparable scope

**primary-evidence**

Genesis 1 describes darkness, deep and waters. Enuma Elish begins with Apsu and Tiamat’s mingled waters. Hesiod places Chaos first. These are three differently worded beginnings.

[Genesis 1: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 1:2](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0101.htm); [The Enuma Elish: Tablets I–III excerpts — Tablet I, opening](https://pages.uoregon.edu/sshoemak/102/texts/enuma.htm); [Theogony — 116–122](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)

## Respect fragments and composite traditions

**historical-interpretation**

The Sumerian flood story opens after a large lacuna. The modern Hermopolitan dossier draws on references such as CT 76, not a surviving continuous epic.

[The Flood story (ETCSL 1.7.4) — Segment A opening](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4); [Gods and goddesses in ancient Egypt: creation — Before creation](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/religion/deitiescreation.html)

## Creation and sovereignty

**historical-interpretation**

Baal’s victory over Yam should not be silently turned into a full account of world creation. The interpretation of divine combat as creation is a scholarly question.

[Le cycle de Baal — KTU 1.2 iv](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/ougarit/fr/le-cycle-de-baal); [Creation at Ugarit and in the Old Testament — pp. 313–324](https://brill.com/previewpdf/journals/vt/15/3/article-p313_3.xml)
