# Staff, rod & scepter

Which similarities survive when we compare the original terms and the objects’ actual roles?

Status: INITIAL DOSSIER.

## Start with the word

**primary-evidence**

Moses’ and Aaron’s implements are called maṭṭeh in the selected Hebrew passages. Hesiod’s gift is a skēptron. The English overlap does not establish that they are the same kind of object.

[Exodus 4: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 4:2–5](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0204.htm); [Exodus 7: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 7:9–12](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0207.htm); [The σκῆπτρον and Odysseus’ kingship in the Odyssey — Theogony discussion](https://classicalstudies.org/%CF%83%CE%BA%CE%B7%CF%80%CF%84%CE%BF%E1%BF%A6%CF%87%CE%BF%CF%82-%CE%B2%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%B9%CE%BB%CE%B5%CF%8D%CF%82-%CF%83%CE%BA%E1%BF%86%CF%80%CF%84%CF%81%CE%BF%CE%BD-and-odysseus%E2%80%99-kingship-odyssey)

## Keep the extraordinary claim

**primary-evidence**

Exodus describes transformations and a staff swallowing rival staffs. Hesiod describes the Muses giving him a staff and voice. Record those claims without replacing them with symbolism or treating them as demonstrated physical events.

[Exodus 4: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 4:2–5, 17](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0204.htm); [Exodus 7: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 7:10–12](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0207.htm); [Theogony — 22–35](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)

## Who acts?

**historical-interpretation**

The object records distinguish wielder, attributed powers and source of power. Hesiod’s divine voice is given by the Muses; the passage does not attribute an autonomous mechanism to the wood.

[Theogony — 30–34](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)

## A physical comparison

**historical-interpretation**

Met 86.1.91 is a curved ivory implement with protective figures. Its association with infant protection and rebirth is the museum’s interpretation; the catalogue title “Magic Wand” does not supply an ancient Egyptian word.

[Magic Wand, 86.1.91 — Description and Artwork Details](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/556561)

## What remains unproved

**speculation**

No cross-cultural identity, identical power source or common origin for these objects is established here. Comparables are research leads, currently speculative.
