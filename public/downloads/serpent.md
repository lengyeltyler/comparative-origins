# Serpents in context

When is a serpent a speaking character, transformed object, image or modern interpretation?

Status: INITIAL DOSSIER.

## Keep the roles separate

**primary-evidence**

Genesis 3 presents a speaking serpent. Exodus 4 describes a staff becoming a serpent. Exodus 7 uses a different Hebrew creature term, tannîn, in the Aaron episode. Similar English renderings should not erase these differences.

[Genesis 3: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 3:1–5](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0103.htm); [Exodus 4: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 4:3–4](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0204.htm); [Exodus 7: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 7:9–12](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0207.htm)

## Interpretations are additional evidence

**speculation**

Later identifications and healing symbolism require their own dated texts or objects. This dossier does not project them into the creation narrative.
