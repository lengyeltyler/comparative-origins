# Flood & survival

What is preserved about destruction, survivors and the aftermath?

Status: PARTIAL COVERAGE.

## Sumerian survival

**primary-evidence**

The Sumerian flood story describes a boat enduring seven days and nights, the return of the sun, sacrifice and Ziusudra’s exceptional life. Its missing stretches must remain visible.

[The Flood story (ETCSL 1.7.4) — Segments D–E](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4)

## The next controlled comparison

**historical-interpretation**

Atrahasis has a flood tradition, represented here by catalogued tablets, but the full episode still needs passage-level collation. Genesis 6–9 and Gilgamesh XI are queued expansions. A complete flood comparison is not claimed.

[Atrahasis III fragment, BM 80385 — Description](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1891-0509-524); [Atrahasis tablet fragment, BM 78943 — Description](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1889-0426-236)

## Historical event questions

**speculation**

A literary flood report and archaeological flood deposits answer different questions. Connecting them requires independent dating, regional evidence and criteria for distinguishing ordinary flooding from a specific claimed event.
