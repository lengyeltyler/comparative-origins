# Divine generations & succession

## The paired passages

**historical-interpretation**

In Hesiod, Kronos attacks Ouranos with a sickle. In the Hittite-Hurrian material summarized by Powell, Kumarbi bites off Anu’s genitals. The distinctive resemblance motivates investigation; the mechanisms differ.
