# The Root Hypothesis

Can surviving traditions be traced toward one earlier common religious or cosmological tradition?

Status: OPEN QUESTION.

## What would count as common ancestry?

**speculation**

A common ancestor is a model to test. It would need a dated chain of evidence and a set of distinctive shared features. Water, darkness or divine beings alone are too general to decide among competing models.

## Evidence for common ancestry

**speculation**

No global common ancestor is established by this initial corpus. The project has not yet assembled the linguistic, archaeological and chronological evidence needed to evaluate that proposition. This is a research status, not a disproof.

## Evidence for borrowing

**historical-interpretation**

The Kumarbi–Hesiod comparison offers a specific research lead involving divine succession and genital violence. The current relationship record is disputed, with moderate confidence in the relevance of comparison. It does not assert direct textual dependence.

[Hesiod: Theogony, Works and Days, The Shield, general introduction — p. 12](https://content.ucpress.edu/chapters/13302.ch01.pdf); [Theogony — 154–182](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)

## Independent development

**speculation**

Test whether ordinary human experiences, environments and political institutions could account for broad patterns. Similarity does not rule out independent development; difference does not establish it.

## Chronology problems

**historical-interpretation**

Composition, oral precursors, earliest attestation and dates of surviving copies must be modeled separately. The Shabaka Stone’s claim to preserve old writing is itself evidence to examine, not a surviving ancient original.

[The Shabako Stone, EA498 — Description; Curator comments](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498)

## Geographical problems

**historical-interpretation**

Ugarit’s dated contact with the Hittite kingdom provides one investigated contact edge. Contact allows a possible route; it does not demonstrate movement of any particular myth. No global genealogical tree is drawn.

[Ugarit — Hittite domination and treaty](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/ugarit)

## Archaeological evidence

**historical-interpretation**

Objects can supply physical dates and contexts. The Egyptian ivory wand has a material history; a literary staff has a narrative history. These are different kinds of records, requiring different tests.

[Magic Wand, 86.1.91 — Artwork Details](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/556561); [Theogony — 22–35](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)

## Missing evidence

**speculation**

Unattested oral traditions, lost manuscripts, uncertain composition dates and uneven excavation leave substantial gaps. A missing link is a research gap; it cannot be filled by drawing an arrow.

## Competing models

**speculation**

Keep five models open: local continuity; borrowing through contact; shared regional inheritance; independent development; a much earlier common source. They need not be mutually exclusive at every scale. For each proposed connection, record what discovery could weaken or strengthen it.
