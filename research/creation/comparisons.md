# Creation comparisons

Original project summaries; source categories and citations are authoritative in corpus.json. TODO is unreviewed, not absence.

## Primordial state

| Text | Category | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Atrahasis | primary-evidence | The opening concerns divine labor, not an origin from nothing. [millard-atra: Tablet I, opening](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/29625/1/10752597.pdf) |
| Ugaritic Baal Cycle | historical-interpretation | No complete world-creation narrative is supplied by the preserved Baal Cycle. The creation significance of combat is debated. [fisher-ugarit: pp. 313–324](https://brill.com/previewpdf/journals/vt/15/3/article-p313_3.xml) |
| Enuma Elish | primary-evidence | Apsu and Tiamat mingle their waters before the naming of heaven and earth. [oregon-enuma: Tablet I, opening](https://pages.uoregon.edu/sshoemak/102/texts/enuma.htm) |
| Eridu Genesis / Sumerian Flood Story | textual-reconstruction | Opening lost; no recoverable continuous account of the primordial state. [etcsl-flood: Segment A, opening lacuna](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4) |
| Genesis 1 (through 2:3) | primary-evidence | Earth is unformed; darkness lies over the deep and waters. [gen-1: 1:2](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0101.htm) |
| Genesis 2–3 (from 2:4) | primary-evidence | No field vegetation yet; rain and a human cultivator are lacking. [gen-2: 2:5–6](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0102.htm) |
| Heliopolitan / Atum material | historical-interpretation | TODO: PT 527 is a generation passage; do not attach an uncited water/void prelude.  |
| Hermopolitan / Ogdoad material | historical-interpretation | Watery expanse, unendingness, darkness and lack of direction in the CT 76 grouping. [ucl-creation: Before creation; CT 76](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/religion/deitiescreation.html) |
| Kumarbi / Song of Emergence | historical-interpretation | The selected account begins with kingship, not the creation of the world. [powell-kumarbi: p. 12](https://content.ucpress.edu/chapters/13302.ch01.pdf) |
| Memphite theology / Shabaka Stone | historical-interpretation | TODO: Collate the surviving hieroglyphic passage; do not supply a reconstructed prelude.  |
| Hesiod’s Theogony | primary-evidence | Chaos comes first, followed by Gaia, Tartarus and Eros. [hesiod: 116–122](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) |

## First actors

| Text | Category | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Atrahasis | primary-evidence | The gods; Enlil, Enki and the birth goddess figure in the human-creation episode. [millard-atra: Tablet I, labor and creation episodes](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/29625/1/10752597.pdf) |
| Ugaritic Baal Cycle | primary-evidence | Baal, Yam and the craftsman Kothar-wa-Hasis in the selected episode. [ugarit-baal: KTU 1.2 iv](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/ougarit/fr/le-cycle-de-baal) |
| Enuma Elish | primary-evidence | Apsu and Tiamat; subsequent generations include Lahmu, Lahamu, Anshar and Kishar. [oregon-enuma: Tablet I, opening genealogy](https://pages.uoregon.edu/sshoemak/102/texts/enuma.htm) |
| Eridu Genesis / Sumerian Flood Story | primary-evidence | An, Enlil, Enki and Ninhursaga fashion people. [etcsl-flood: A 10–14](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4) |
| Genesis 1 (through 2:3) | primary-evidence | God creates and speaks. [gen-1: 1:1–3](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0101.htm) |
| Genesis 2–3 (from 2:4) | primary-evidence | YHWH God forms the human. [gen-2: 2:7](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0102.htm) |
| Heliopolitan / Atum material | primary-evidence | Atum, followed by Shu and Tefnut. [pt-allen: PT 527 (§1248)](https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AncientEgyptianPyramid_Allen_SBL.pdf) |
| Hermopolitan / Ogdoad material | historical-interpretation | Four pairs; CT 76 includes Tenem and Tenemet, not the Amun pair familiar from later lists. [ucl-creation: Before creation; CT 76](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/religion/deitiescreation.html) |
| Kumarbi / Song of Emergence | historical-interpretation | Alalu, Anu and Kumarbi occupy the succession sequence. [powell-kumarbi: p. 12](https://content.ucpress.edu/chapters/13302.ch01.pdf) |
| Memphite theology / Shabaka Stone | historical-interpretation | Ptah is assigned priority in creation. [bm-shabaka: Description; Curator comments](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498) |
| Hesiod’s Theogony | primary-evidence | Chaos, Gaia, Tartarus and Eros; Gaia later bears Ouranos. [hesiod: 116–132](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) |

## Creation / order

| Text | Category | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Atrahasis | primary-evidence | Humans take over divine labor after the gods protest. [millard-atra: Tablet I, labor dispute](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/29625/1/10752597.pdf) |
| Ugaritic Baal Cycle | primary-evidence | Kothar makes named clubs; Baal strikes and defeats Yam. [ugarit-baal: KTU 1.2 iv](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/ougarit/fr/le-cycle-de-baal) |
| Enuma Elish | primary-evidence | Marduk splits Tiamat’s body and makes a sky-roof from one half, containing her waters. [heidel: Enuma Elish IV 137–140 (printed p. 42)](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/misc_genesis.pdf) |
| Eridu Genesis / Sumerian Flood Story | primary-evidence | People and animals are made; cities and kingship follow. [etcsl-flood: A 10–14; B 6–18](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4) |
| Genesis 1 (through 2:3) | primary-evidence | Speech, separation and naming structure the account. [gen-1: 1:3–10](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0101.htm) |
| Genesis 2–3 (from 2:4) | primary-evidence | Forming, breathing, planting and building describe divine actions. [gen-2: 2:7–9, 21–22](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0102.htm) |
| Heliopolitan / Atum material | primary-evidence | Atum masturbates; Shu and Tefnut are born. [pt-allen: PT 527 (§1248)](https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AncientEgyptianPyramid_Allen_SBL.pdf) |
| Hermopolitan / Ogdoad material | historical-interpretation | TODO: Collate CT 76 and later accounts before assembling an emergence sequence.  |
| Kumarbi / Song of Emergence | historical-interpretation | Kumarbi bites off Anu’s genitals and becomes pregnant. [powell-kumarbi: p. 12](https://content.ucpress.edu/chapters/13302.ch01.pdf) |
| Memphite theology / Shabaka Stone | historical-interpretation | The catalogue identifies creation through the spoken word. [bm-shabaka: Description](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498) |
| Hesiod’s Theogony | primary-evidence | Divine generation and struggles over rule organize the genealogy. [hesiod: 116–210; 453–506](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) |

## Divine structure

| Text | Category | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Atrahasis | primary-evidence | The assembly approves the creation plan. [millard-atra: Tablet I, iv 49–57](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/29625/1/10752597.pdf) |
| Ugaritic Baal Cycle | primary-evidence | The episode concerns Baal’s claim to kingship. [ugarit-baal: KTU 1.2 iv](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/ougarit/fr/le-cycle-de-baal) |
| Enuma Elish | primary-evidence | The gods assemble and establish Marduk’s authority. [oregon-enuma: Tablets II–III](https://pages.uoregon.edu/sshoemak/102/texts/enuma.htm) |
| Eridu Genesis / Sumerian Flood Story | primary-evidence | A divine assembly decrees the flood. [etcsl-flood: C 1–27](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=t.1.7.4) |
| Genesis 1 (through 2:3) | primary-evidence | A plural deliberation appears at 1:26; the referent of “us” requires interpretation. [gen-1: 1:26](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0101.htm) |
| Genesis 2–3 (from 2:4) | primary-evidence | The text says the human has become “like one of us”; identities are not enumerated. [gen-3: 3:22](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0103.htm) |
| Heliopolitan / Atum material | primary-evidence | Two divine offspring arise from Atum. [pt-allen: PT 527 (§1248)](https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AncientEgyptianPyramid_Allen_SBL.pdf) |
| Hermopolitan / Ogdoad material | historical-interpretation | The Eight are a pre-creation grouping, not a single continuous Hermopolitan epic. [ucl-creation: Before creation](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/religion/deitiescreation.html) |
| Kumarbi / Song of Emergence | historical-interpretation | Anu overthrows Alalu; Kumarbi attacks Anu. [powell-kumarbi: p. 12](https://content.ucpress.edu/chapters/13302.ch01.pdf) |
| Memphite theology / Shabaka Stone | historical-interpretation | Ptah is credited with creating the other gods. [bm-shabaka: Jones 1990a curator comment](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498) |
| Hesiod’s Theogony | primary-evidence | Kronos attacks Ouranos; Zeus’s generation later fights the Titans. [hesiod: 154–182; 617–735](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) |
