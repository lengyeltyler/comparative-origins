# The making of humanity

Which materials, actions and purposes are actually named?

Status: INITIAL COMPARISON.

## Three materials, distinct descriptions

**primary-evidence**

Genesis 2:7 specifies ground-dust and the breath of life. Atrahasis’s creation passage mixes clay with divine flesh and blood. Enuma Elish VI names Qingu’s blood and the transfer of divine work to humanity.

[Genesis 2: Hebrew text and JPS 1917 translation — 2:7](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0102.htm); [The Atrahasis Epic and its place in Babylonian literature — Tablet I, iv 39–57](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/29625/1/10752597.pdf); [The Babylonian Genesis: The Story of Creation — Enuma Elish VI 29–36](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/misc_genesis.pdf)

## Do not turn a tag into a translation

**speculation**

“Humanity from earth” and “humanity from clay” remain distinct motifs. Material similarity is relevant, but a claim of borrowing requires additional evidence.

## The scope of Hesiod’s account

**primary-evidence**

The Theogony describes the fashioning of the first woman in Zeus’s response to Prometheus. It does not provide a symmetric origin account for all humans in this passage.

[Theogony — 570–612](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)
