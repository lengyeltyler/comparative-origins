## Other Phase 1 limitations

- The majority of composition and earliest-witness fields remain undated pending defensible review. Timeline omissions are deliberate.
- Four object dossiers are a seed, not an exhaustive staff census. Original Egyptian object naming and all earliest-type attestations remain open.
- The contact register has one sourced edge. A comprehensive chronological network is not yet populated; no unsupported ancestry diagram is drawn.
- Relationship classifications are fully supported by the model; only two proposals are entered. Neither establishes a single universal root.
- Religious reception is represented by one attributed Genesis example. A broad reception history requires additional dated sources.
- Source availability checks cannot replace expert verification. Specialist review, correction history and formal reviewer sign-off remain to be implemented before using `reviewed` dossier status.
- Full original texts, transliterations and modern critical apparatus are not mirrored. Copyright and licensing must be resolved before importing them.
- The static interface requires JavaScript; no-script access is through the downloadable evidence and cited tables. Search is local substring/token matching, without linguistic stemming.
- Automated render checks do not replace interactive browser/accessibility testing. Such testing was not explicitly requested in this build.
